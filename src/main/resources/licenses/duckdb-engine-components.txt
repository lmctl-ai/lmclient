DuckDB engine source and bundled third-party components
=======================================================

The native DuckDB JNI library is built from duckdb/duckdb-java at commit
679d526542cf9dfb24322d9d9c75641624f24bc1 (the source pin is recorded in
deps.lock). The engine is MIT licensed; see duckdb-mit.txt.

The pinned engine source includes these third-party components. Their complete
license texts are included once in this directory under the named file:
